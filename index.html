Background

About Joanna

Joanna can be heard on radio stations throughout the US and Canada, as well as on promos for television, commercials, and narration. She has voiced promos for The Today Show, NBC Nightly News, CBS, and Extra.

Her radio imaging clientele ranges in format from Hot AC all the way to Sports Talk. Narration credits include shows on DIY Network and TLC.

Joanna's home studio is located in San Diego, CA. She is able to serve clients quickly while delivering the high level of professionalism and quality you expect. Her craft has established her as one of the top female voice over artists working today.

Represented by Atlas Talent Agency in New York and Los Angeles.
